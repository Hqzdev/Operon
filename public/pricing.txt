# Pricing — Operon

Last updated: 2026-05-08

## Starter (Free)

- Price: $0/month
- Credit card required: No
- Analyses: 10 campaign analyses per month
- Users: 1

### Included features
- Scale / Fix / Kill / Test Again verdict on every analysis
- Root cause diagnosis (creative, offer, or product)
- 3 actionable next steps per verdict
- Decision history (recent analyses)
- Manual data entry (enter campaign numbers directly)
- Chrome extension (auto-fill from Meta or TikTok Ads Manager)

---

## Basic

- Price: $9/month (billed monthly)
- Credit card required: Yes
- Analyses: Unlimited
- Users: 1

### Included features
- Everything in Starter
- Unlimited campaign analyses
- Full decision history (all past verdicts)
- Priority processing

---

## Pro

- Price: $19/month (billed monthly)
- Credit card required: Yes
- Analyses: Unlimited
- Users: 1

### Included features
- Everything in Basic
- Budget Allocation tool (spread budget across ad sets by ROAS)
- Scenario Simulator (preview ROAS and revenue if budget changes)
- Weekly digest email (summary of all campaign decisions)
- Autopilot (Chrome extension scans open Ads Manager tabs every 24h, notifies on decision changes)

---

## Notes

- No credit card required for Starter plan
- Cancel anytime, no long-term contracts
- Plans are per-user (individual seller workspace)
- Payments processed securely via major credit and debit cards
- Access starts immediately after payment
